# Agent-Research

**Reports from large-scale, agent-driven research and analysis passes over the
project.** Where an [epic page](../epics/index.md) tells the story of a *build*
and an [ADR](../../decisions/index.md) records a *decision*, an Agent-Research
page records the output of a **fan-out research task** — many agents reading
the codebase and the literature in parallel to map a design space, survey
prior art, or triage a set of directions — curated into a single durable
reference.

These pages are a distinct lens from the other records, so nothing is
duplicated (see [Records & canon](../../process/records-and-canon.md#the-record-lenses)): this
section holds the **map or survey**; the [epics](../epics/index.md) hold build
stories; the [changelog](../changelog.md) is the diary; the
[decisions registry](../../decisions/index.md) and topic docs hold canonical
state; the [open questions](../../direction/open-questions.md) are the live Q-registry
these passes feed.

A report here is a **snapshot in time**: it reflects the codebase and
literature as of its date, and it *proposes and rates* — **it does not
decide**. Anything it surfaces that becomes load-bearing graduates into the
appropriate canonical home (an ADR, an epic, a topic page, or a new open
question); the report stays as the durable record of how the direction was
found and why it was rated the way it was. New reports start from
`docs/.templates/agent-research-report.md`.
